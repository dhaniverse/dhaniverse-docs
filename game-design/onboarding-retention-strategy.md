# Dhaniverse: Onboarding & Retention Strategy

## Executive Summary

This document outlines Dhaniverse's user onboarding and retention strategy, designed specifically for WCHL National Round judging criteria focusing on **traction, daily active users, and competitive differentiation**.

### Key Objectives

-   **Immediate Hook**: Engage users within first 60 seconds
-   **Daily Habit Formation**: Create compelling reasons to return daily
-   **Retention Optimization**: Prevent churn through strategic interventions
-   **Competitive Differentiation**: Unique value propositions vs. existing games

---

## Phase 1: Critical First Impression (0-5 Minutes)

### The "Welcome to Your Financial Journey" Hook

```
User Flow:
1. Character selection (C1-C4) - "Choose your avatar for this adventure!"
2. Spawn in town square with welcoming NPC: "Welcome to Dhaniverse!"
3. Tutorial NPC: "I'm Maya, your financial guide. Ready to build your wealth?"
4. First gift: "Here's 1,000 rupees to start your journey!"
5. Guided walk: "Let's explore your new city - see that building? That's the bank!"
6. Simple interaction: "Talk to the banker to learn about saving money"
7. First deposit: "Put 500 rupees in savings - watch it grow!"
8. Immediate feedback: "Great! You'll earn 50 rupees interest by tomorrow"
9. Achievement unlock: "Smart Saver - Made your first deposit"
```

### Success Metrics

-   **Time to First Win**: < 60 seconds
-   **Initial Engagement**: 100% guided completion
-   **Emotional Response**: Excitement from immediate profit

## Specific Tutorial Sequences

### Tutorial 1: "Your First Investment Adventure"

```
Objective: Gentle introduction to investing with guaranteed success

Script:
1. NPC Maya: "Now that you've learned about saving, let's try investing!"
2. Walk to Stock Exchange: "This building is where companies sell shares"
3. NPC Broker (friendly): "Welcome! I have a great beginner stock for you"
4. Stock Introduction: "SUNSHINE Corp makes solar panels - very popular lately!"
5. Current price: "Each share costs 10 rupees - perfect for beginners"
6. Guided Action: "Let's buy 10 shares with your remaining 500 rupees"
7. Immediate positive feedback: "Great choice! This company is growing"
8. Small price increase: Stock goes from 10 to 12 rupees (20% gain)
9. Profit celebration: "Congratulations! You made 20 rupees profit!"
10. Achievement: "First Investor - Made your first stock purchase"
11. Lesson: "You just learned how investing in good companies can grow your money"

Duration: 2 minutes
Success Rate Target: 100% (fully guided)
Emotional Impact: Accomplishment, curiosity, "This is fun!"
```

### Tutorial 2: "The Compound Effect"

```
Objective: Introduce banking and passive income concepts

Script:
1. NPC Banker: "Smart investors make their money work for them"
2. Guided Action: "Deposit your $500 profit in our savings account"
3. Real-time Demo: Watch balance grow from $500 to $505 in 1 minute
4. Explanation: "That's compound interest - your money makes money while you sleep"
5. Challenge: "Set a reminder to check back in 1 hour"
6. Achievement: "Passive Income Starter"
7. Push Notification Setup: "Get alerts when your money grows"

Duration: 90 seconds
Learning Outcome: Understanding of passive income
Retention Hook: Reason to return in 1 hour
```

### Tutorial 3: "Social Trading Power"

```
Objective: Introduce social features and competitive elements

Script:
1. NPC: "The best traders learn from each other"
2. Feature Introduction: "See what successful players are buying"
3. Leaderboard: "You're currently ranked #1,247 out of 10,000 players"
4. Friend System: "Invite friends to compete and learn together"
5. Copy Trading: "Follow top trader's moves automatically"
6. Achievement: "Social Butterfly - Added first friend"
7. Challenge: "Beat your friend's portfolio performance this week"

Duration: 2 minutes
Social Hook: Friend competition
Viral Mechanism: Friend invitations
```

## Retention Metrics & KPIs

### Primary Metrics

-   **D1 Retention**: % of users who return the next day (Target: 40%+)
-   **D7 Retention**: % of users who return after 7 days (Target: 20%+)
-   **D30 Retention**: % of users who return after 30 days (Target: 10%+)
-   **Session Length**: Average time per session (Target: 15+ minutes)
-   **Sessions per Day**: How often users return (Target: 3+ sessions)

### Engagement Metrics

-   **Actions per Session**: Trades, deposits, social interactions (Target: 5+ actions)
-   **Feature Adoption Rate**: % using banking, stocks, Web3 features
-   **Social Engagement**: Friend adds, challenges, leaderboard climbs
-   **Achievement Completion**: % of available achievements unlocked
-   **Streak Maintenance**: Consecutive daily login streaks

### Business Metrics

-   **Time to Web3**: Days from signup to first blockchain transaction
-   **Conversion Rate**: % of users who complete Web3 onboarding
-   **Viral Coefficient**: Average invites sent per user
-   **Lifetime Value**: Total engagement time and feature usage
-   **Churn Recovery**: % of churned users who return after intervention

## Conclusion

This onboarding and retention strategy transforms Dhaniverse from a simple finance game into a **habit-forming, educational, and socially engaging platform** that naturally progresses users from traditional finance concepts to Web3 mastery.

The key innovation is the **progressive disclosure of complexity** - users start with familiar concepts and gradually unlock advanced features as they demonstrate mastery and engagement. This approach maximizes both immediate traction and long-term retention while providing clear competitive differentiation in the market.

By focusing on **immediate gratification, social competition, and continuous learning**, Dhaniverse creates a compelling user experience that judges will recognize as having genuine traction potential and sustainable user engagement.
